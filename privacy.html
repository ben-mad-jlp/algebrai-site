AlgebrAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

AlgebrAI is an algebra learning app. We don't run accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we (the developer) don't receive any data about you.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, settings, the equations you type in the Graph Lab, the problems you ask about and their lessons are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Your AI key, if you add one, is stored in the device Keychain.

The optional AI tutor

If a parent adds their own API key for an AI service (xAI, OpenAI or Anthropic), then when the student asks the tutor about a problem, the app sends that problem — the typed text and/or the photo they choose, plus the graph — directly from the device to that service, using that key, so it can explain the problem. It isn't sent to us. That service's own privacy policy applies to what it receives. Without a key, nothing leaves the device.

Links to the App Store

The app can suggest our other apps. Tapping one opens its App Store page, and Apple's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects no personal information. The AI tutor is off until a parent adds a key.
