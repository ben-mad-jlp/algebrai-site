AlgebrAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

AlgebrAI is an algebra learning app. We don't run accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we (the developer) don't receive any data about you.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, settings and the equations you type in the Graph Lab are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Links to the App Store

The app can suggest our other apps. Tapping one opens its App Store page, and Apple's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects no personal information.
