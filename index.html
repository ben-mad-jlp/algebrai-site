Common questions

Do I need an account or internet? No. Every topic, example, practice problem and the Graph Lab work offline with no account.

How do I change a graph? Drag the dots on the curve. The equation on the graph rewrites itself as you move them.

How do I type an answer? Use the keypad under the problem. Answers like 3/4, 2√5, x^2 - 9 and 3 + 2i are accepted, and close slips get a hint.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.
